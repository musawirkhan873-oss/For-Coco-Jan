My dearest Saira,

There are words that live deep inside us — words so sacred, so heavy with love, that ordinary language cannot carry them. Yet here I am, reaching for every syllable that might come close to describing what you mean to me.

From the very first moment I truly saw you — not just your face, but your soul — something shifted in my universe that has never shifted back. Nor would I ever want it to.

You carry within you a warmth so rare, a grace so effortless, a strength so quiet and so magnificent, that I wake every morning feeling like the luckiest man to have ever walked this earth — simply because I get to call you mine.

You are my sunrise and my evening star. My laughter and my peace. The answer to every prayer I whispered before I even knew your name.

I promise to love you in all your seasons — when you bloom, when you are weary, when the world is loud and when it goes quiet. I promise to choose you, every single day, in every version of myself I will ever become.

Today I am not just giving you these words. I am giving you my entire heart — every corner, every crack, every hidden room. It has always belonged to you.

💍
Forever yours — Musawir Khan Achakzai
